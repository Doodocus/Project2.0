## Selected research

All titles, venues, findings, and collaborators below are fictional. Treat these as portfolio mock data, not real publications.

### 1. “Evidence Before Fluency: Measuring Unsupported Claims in Retrieval-Augmented Language Models”
- **Status:** Fictional undergraduate research paper; submitted to a fictional student research workshop, 2026
- **Contribution:** Co-designed a test set of questions where retrieved documents contain incomplete, conflicting, or irrelevant evidence. Compared answer correctness with whether each answer’s claims were actually supported by the retrieved sources.
- **Takeaway:** A fluent answer can still overstate what its sources establish; claim-level evidence checks make that gap easier to detect.

### 2. “Prompt Injection Across the Tool Boundary: A Small-Scale Study of Agent Defenses”
- **Status:** Fictional preprint, 2025
- **Contribution:** Built a controlled set of indirect prompt-injection scenarios for tool-using language-model agents and compared simple isolation and tool-permission strategies.
- **Takeaway:** Narrow tool permissions and explicit separation of untrusted content reduced successful attacks in the study’s toy environment.

### 3. “When ‘I Don’t Know’ Helps: Calibrating Abstention in Student-Facing Assistants”
- **Status:** Fictional poster, MIT Undergraduate Research Symposium, 2025
- **Contribution:** Evaluated when an educational assistant should answer, cite a source, or abstain, using a small synthetic question set and human review rubric.
- **Takeaway:** Abstention quality depends on giving users a useful next step, not simply refusing uncertain questions.

### 4. “A Practical Taxonomy of Hallucination Reports for Product Teams”
- **Status:** Fictional technical report, 2024
- **Contribution:** Organized common user-reported failures into categories such as unsupported detail, source mismatch, stale information, and false certainty.
- **Takeaway:** A consistent taxonomy can help teams prioritize fixes and compare failures across product releases.
